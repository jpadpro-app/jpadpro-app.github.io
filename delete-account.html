Delete your JPad Pro account

App: JPad Pro · Developer: JPadPro

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Open JPad Pro and tap the menu (☰).
  2. Tap Account (your email address).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with OK.

Option 2 — by email

Send an email to jpadprojdev@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my JPad Pro account” from the email address of your account. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, account ID and sign-in data.
  • The link between your account and your Pro subscription.

What is kept

  • Purchase records (date, product, amount) are kept by Google Play and by our subscription provider RevenueCat as required for tax, accounting and fraud prevention. They are no longer linked to your deleted JPadPro account.

What is not affected

  • Documents on your phone — they stay until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Files in your own Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive — they belong to you and stay there.
  • An active Google Play subscription is not cancelled automatically. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
